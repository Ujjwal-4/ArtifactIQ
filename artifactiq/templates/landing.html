Forensic principles built in

◈ Original evidence is never written to: preserved copies live under randomized names and are SHA-256 hashed at registration.
◈ Append-only, hash-chained custody records; verification recomputes the whole chain.
◈ Live acquisition is explicit, targeted and audited — access-denied is reported as access-denied, never silently worked around.
◈ Missing timestamps stay missing; events with untrustworthy time are labeled, not fudged.
◈ Branches, risk scores and ATT&CK mappings expose their formula, inputs and rule version.
◈ Local-first: nothing leaves the workstation unless an LLM provider is explicitly enabled.
